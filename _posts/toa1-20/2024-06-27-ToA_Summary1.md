## Synopsis

The ship carrying Gronk and Vati approached Port Nyanzaru before dawn. As the ship docked, Vati spent a moment savoring his newfound freedom after 15 years of imprisonment, before going below deck to wake Gronk, Adonai, and Nicolau. The group discussed their plans. Gronk shared the knowledge he gained over the course of the voyage concerning survival in Chult, emphasizing the need to take on questionable jobs for survival. Collyn, a journalist, interrupted their conversation, probing about their past, but Gronk and Vati evaded her questions. Collyn suggested they find a sponsor and mentioned a bulletin board near the quartermaster’s building.

Cid, a blacksmith, encountered Gronk and Vati at the job board. They discussed the impracticality of their armor in Chult's heat and the possibility of joining Cid in an expedition. The group, joined by Snookie Longpocket, a gnome looking for work, explored the city. Snookie was later revealed to be a pervert, leading Vati to report him to a bathhouse attendant.

Cid met with Syndra, who offered him a partially complete map of Chult, urging caution when revealing said map around others due to the rarity. Meanwhile, Gronk and Vati relaxed at the bathhouse after the long journey to Port Nyanzaru from Amn. They eventually regrouped with Cid at a café, where they discussed finding a new guide. The group then headed to the Thundering Lizard inn, securing rooms before searching for Undril, a potential employer for their expedition.


## Notes
- The map Syndra gave Cid was a gift from herself, Bayle, and Nahia. The map itself is from a time when Bayle and Syndra travelled together, and it features recently marked landmarks and zoning notes from the guide and cartographer Nahia comissioned to restore and update the map.
- Most paraphernalia dedicated to foreign gods will be found outside the main city.
- Not everyone in Chult speaks common. Only those who deal with foreigners often have the incentive to learn and be fluent in the language.
- Vati has a habit of addressing people as "young" and showing his age. When meeting Cid he addressed the blacksmith as "young man" twice, and called the 50-year-old Snookie "little fella".
